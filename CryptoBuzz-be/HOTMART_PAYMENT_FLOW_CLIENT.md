# Hotmart Payment Integration - Client Overview

## How It Works (Simple Explanation)

Your CryptoBuzz platform is now integrated with Hotmart payment system. Here's how the payment process works when a student wants to buy one of your paid courses:

### Step 1: Student Clicks "Buy Course"
When a student finds a premium course they want to purchase, they click the "Buy" or "Purchase" button.

### Step 2: Student Goes to Hotmart Payment Page
The student is redirected to Hotmart's secure payment page (just like PayPal or Stripe). They can pay using:
- Credit or Debit Cards
- PayPal
- Bank Transfer
- And many other payment methods supported by Hotmart

### Step 3: Student Completes Payment
The student enters their payment information and completes the purchase on Hotmart's secure payment page.

### Step 4: Student Gets Instant Access
Once payment is successful, Hotmart automatically notifies your platform, and the student immediately gets access to the course. They can start learning right away!

### Step 5: Everything is Tracked Automatically
All purchases are automatically recorded in your system. You can see:
- Who bought which course
- When they purchased it
- Payment status
- Course access status

## What You Need to Do

### Before Launch:
1. **Create Products in Hotmart** - For each paid course, create a product in your Hotmart dashboard and get the Product ID
2. **Link Courses to Products** - Connect each course in your platform to its Hotmart Product ID
3. **Set Up Webhook** - Configure Hotmart to notify your platform when payments are completed (one-time setup)

### Daily Operations:
- **Nothing!** The system works automatically. When students purchase courses, they get instant access without any manual steps from you.

## Common Scenarios

### Student Buys a Course
1. Student purchases → Payment processed by Hotmart → Student gets instant access ✅

### Student Gets Refund
1. Refund processed in Hotmart → System automatically removes course access → Student can no longer access the course ✅

### Student Tries to Access Free Course
1. Student clicks course → System checks → Course is free → Student gets instant access ✅ (no payment needed)

### Student Tries to Access Paid Course They Haven't Bought
1. Student clicks course → System checks → No purchase found → Student sees "Purchase Required" message ✅
